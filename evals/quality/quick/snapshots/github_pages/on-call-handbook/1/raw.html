On-call handbook

Kestrel pages the on-call engineer through the PagerDuty service kestrel-primary.

Responding to a page

  • Acknowledge a page within 5 minutes.
  • If the primary does not acknowledge within 15 minutes, the page escalates to the secondary on-call engineer.
  • Write the incident summary in the #kestrel-incidents channel before the shift ends.
